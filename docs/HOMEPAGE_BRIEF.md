# Homepage direction contract

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Software as considered craft: let the supplied introduction, authentic project evidence, and practical product purposes carry the page.

**OWN-WORLD:** The user's Copper Atelier palette and three fonts, original MA monogram, flat architectural geometry, restrained rules, and a warm dark close.

**STORY:** Meet Anees, examine a current private project and three public projects, understand his interests, then make direct contact.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Leading serif statement and working CTAs opposite a large neutral geometric study; greeting above the supplied headline, current project beneath the actions, technologies below the composition. Stack in that order on mobile.

**FORM:** User-approved editorial two-column portfolio; code-led. Seed: none (user supplied the approved direction and requested implementation).

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Scope and mode

Experience. Phase 1 only; the user's master brief is the approved direction. Code-first implementation, with no generated character assets or alternative design proposal. Seed: none (the user supplied and approved the visual direction).

## Audience and action

Recruiters and collaborators should understand what Anees builds, examine public software repositories, and contact him. Primary hero action: Explore my work. Secondary: Get to know me.

## First viewport

A generous editorial split: expressive serif statement on the leading side; precise abstract copper geometry within a portrait-sized study on the trailing side. A small personal greeting, readable introduction, two actions, and a current-project link. The artwork is explicitly a portrait placeholder, never a fabricated likeness. Mobile preserves reading order and stacks the study after the introduction.

## Structure and rhythm

Refined navigation; hero; a concise development-interest line; selected work with a large SportLynk feature and three smaller public-project previews; spacious introductory About with compact engineering and journey anchors; a dark contact close. Avoid repetitive boxed sections. Ask Anees links to an honest planned-feature note, with email as the useful alternative.

## Signature motion

A single coordinated hero entrance in semantic groups, using Motion for React, transform and opacity, the shared exponential ease-out, and a short stagger. One-time project-media reveals support exploration. Interface controls respond quickly; keyboard interactions remain immediate; reduced motion uses opacity only. No looping geometry, parallax, or fabricated character animation.

## Proof and open decisions

Project facts come from the user and the three public project READMEs/manifests. Real screenshots, when available, keep their native product colors. SportLynk's visual is a labeled conceptual court, not an invented application screen. Detailed project status and personal contributions need deeper review in Phase 3. Character photo and artwork require Phase 2 approval.
