# SportLynk

SportLynk is Muhammad Anees's team Final Year Project, focused on sports venue booking and competitive matchmaking. It is a private project currently in development. SportLynk has not launched.

## Product direction

The approved public case study explains venue discovery, booking and competitive matchmaking as product direction. The product map and court artwork are conceptual. They are not verified implementation diagrams or application screenshots.

## Contribution and implementation boundaries

Anees is developing SportLynk with his Final Year Project team. The public portfolio does not establish individual task ownership, teammates' specific work, implemented-versus-planned feature lists or the private technology stack. Do not attribute all team work to Anees or infer a backend framework, database, rating algorithm or deployment from the product concept.

## Status and outcomes

SportLynk remains in development, not launched. No verified users, customers, revenue, downloads, production deployment or measured business outcomes are published. The portfolio's Concurrency Lab is an independent simplified educational demonstration, not evidence of SportLynk's backend.
